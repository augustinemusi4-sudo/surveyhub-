About SurveyHub Kenya

SurveyHub Kenya is an online questionnaire platform designed to make it easy for registered members to access and complete surveys.

Our goal is to provide a simple and user-friendly survey experience.

Members pay a one-time KSh 50 access fee. Payment terms and privacy information should be reviewed before registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I join?

Create an account using your name, email and phone number.


2. How much is registration?

The planned one-time access fee is KSh 50.


3. How do I pay?

M-Pesa integration is still being configured. Do not send money until payment is officially enabled.


4. Will I earn money by answering surveys?

No earnings are promised by this platform. The current membership fee is for survey access.

Yes. The website is designed to work on smartphones and computers.
