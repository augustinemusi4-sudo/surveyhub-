About SurveyHub Kenya

SurveyHub Kenya is a survey participation platform designed to allow registered members to answer questionnaires and share their opinions.

Surveys may cover different topics depending on what is published on the platform.

SurveyHub does not promise employment, earnings, or guaranteed financial rewards for completing surveys.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is SurveyHub Kenya?

It is a platform for registered members to participate in available surveys.

2. How much is membership access?

The proposed one-time access fee is KSh 50.

3. Will I earn money by answering surveys?

No. Members are not paid for answering surveys.

4. How do I register?

Open the registration page, enter your details and create an account.

5. Is M-Pesa payment available?

M-Pesa integration is not yet configured.

6. Can I view my previous responses?

Yes. Your submitted responses should appear in the response history in your dashboard.
